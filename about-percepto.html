Percepto

A Digital Visual-Perception Screening Platform for Early Identification of Cerebral Visual Impairment (CVI) and related visual processing difficulties.

The Problem

Cerebral Visual Impairment (CVI) is frequently underdiagnosed. Children with CVI may appear to see normally during casual observation while struggling significantly with visual processing tasks.

  • Limited Access: Traditional assessments require specialized equipment and trained professionals
  • Specialist Shortage: Pediatric ophthalmologists and CVI specialists are scarce, particularly in underserved regions
  • Delayed Identification: Many at-risk children never receive evaluation, delaying critical early intervention
  • Cost Barriers: Existing tools are expensive, limiting deployment in schools and community programs

Early identification can significantly improve outcomes through targeted interventions, accommodations, and therapy.

Our Solution

Percepto transforms visual-perception assessment into structured, interactive, gamified tasks.

Gamified Assessments

Child-friendly, engaging interfaces that reduce assessment anxiety. Interactive tasks across 12 visual-perception domains with immediate feedback.

Structured Data Collection

Quantitative metrics on accuracy, response time, and interaction patterns. Multi-session tracking to monitor changes over time.

Screening & Referral Support

Systematic visual-perception profiling across multiple domains. Generates professional reports for clinical review.

Scalable Deployment

Browser-based platform requiring no specialized hardware. Deployable via GitHub Pages, Firebase, or institutional servers.

Child-Friendly Experience

Designed with CVI-friendly colors and contrasts. Engaging visuals and positive reinforcement reduce assessment fatigue.

What Percepto Measures

Percepto collects structured behavioral metrics during assessment completion:

Performance Metrics

  • Accuracy: Percentage of correct responses
  • Response Time: Time for individual items
  • Average Time: Mean response across assessment
  • Error Patterns: Types and consistency of mistakes

Behavioral Patterns

  • Total Taps/Clicks: Interaction frequency
  • Response Patterns: Sequence and timing
  • Attention Indicators: Sustained engagement
  • First Tap Time: Initial response latency

Report Outputs

  • Individual scores with interpretation bands
  • Category-wise performance summaries
  • Progress tracking across sessions
  • Visual charts for clinical review

Important: These metrics support screening and profiling. They do not constitute a medical diagnosis.

Assessment Categories

Percepto evaluates 12 core visual perception domains through specialized interactive assessments:

Visual Discrimination

Ability to identify differences and similarities between shapes, colors, sizes, and patterns. Essential for reading and mathematics.

Visual Closure

Ability to recognize objects when parts are missing or obscured. Important for object recognition in clutter.

Visual Form Constancy

Ability to recognize forms regardless of size, color, position, or orientation. Critical for reading different fonts.

Visual Memory

Ability to remember visual information and recall it later. Important for copying tasks and spelling.

Visual Sequential Memory

Ability to remember sequences of visual stimuli in order. Critical for daily living skills and following instructions.

Visual Spatial Skills

Ability to understand spatial relationships and navigate space. Important for map reading and sports.

Visual Neglect

Assessment of visual field awareness and attention to both sides of space. Important for post-stroke screening.

Figure Ground

Ability to distinguish objects from their background. Essential for visual scanning and reading.

Motion Perception

Ability to detect and track moving objects. Important for sports participation and safe navigation.

Pursuit & Fixation

Eye movement control and visual attention. Assesses oculomotor control for reading and attention.

Observation Tasks

Structured behavioral observation for comprehensive functional vision profiling and environmental assessment.

Biological Motion

Recognition of human and animal movement patterns from motion-based stimuli. Essential for social interaction.

Clinical Positioning

What Percepto Is

  • A screening tool for visual-perception abilities
  • A profiling platform for identifying strengths and challenges
  • A referral support system for clinical decision-making
  • A progress tracking tool for monitoring over time
  • A visual-perception assessment aid for structured evaluation

What Percepto Is NOT

  • A diagnostic device or engine
  • A medical device requiring regulatory approval
  • A replacement for clinical decision-making
  • A substitute for comprehensive ophthalmological evaluation
  • A standalone diagnostic for CVI or other conditions
Clinical Disclaimer

Percepto is a visual perception screening and profiling aid. It is not a diagnostic device. Results should be interpreted by a qualified clinician. These assessments are designed for screening purposes only and do not replace comprehensive clinical evaluation.

Appropriate Use Cases

✓ Appropriate:
  • Initial screening to identify children needing specialist evaluation
  • Visual-perception profiling to guide intervention planning
  • Progress monitoring in response to therapy
  • Research data collection with ethics approval
  • Educational assessment for learning support
✗ Not Appropriate:
  • Sole basis for medical diagnosis
  • Replacement for comprehensive clinical evaluation
  • Determining eligibility for services alone
  • Self-diagnosis by families or caregivers

Current Status

Percepto is currently a pilot-stage prototype. It is suitable for demonstrations, research studies with ethics approval, and small-scale pilot programs.

Not Clinically Validated

  • No normative data established
  • Not validated against gold-standard assessments
  • Not peer-reviewed through clinical publication
  • Not regulatory approved (CE, FDA, etc.)

Technical Capabilities

  • Firebase integration for authentication and data storage

Clinical validation studies are planned. The platform is ready for pilot testing to gather feedback and validate usability.
