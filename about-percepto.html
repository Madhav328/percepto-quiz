Percepto

A Digital Visual-Perception Screening Platform for Early Identification of Cerebral Visual Impairment (CVI) and related visual processing difficulties.

The Problem

Cerebral Visual Impairment (CVI) is frequently underdiagnosed. Children with CVI may appear to see normally during casual observation while struggling significantly with visual processing tasks.

  • Limited Access: Traditional assessments require specialized equipment and trained professionals
  • Specialist Shortage: Pediatric ophthalmologists and CVI specialists are scarce, particularly in underserved regions
  • Delayed Identification: Many at-risk children never receive evaluation, delaying critical early intervention
  • Cost Barriers: Existing tools are expensive, limiting deployment in schools and community programs

Early identification can significantly improve outcomes through targeted interventions, accommodations, and therapy.

Assessment Categories

Percepto evaluates 11 core visual perception domains through specialized interactive assessments:

Visual Discrimination

Ability to identify differences and similarities between shapes, colors, sizes, and patterns. Essential for reading and mathematics.

Visual Closure

Ability to recognize objects when parts are missing or obscured. Important for object recognition in clutter.

Visual Form Constancy

Ability to recognize forms regardless of size, color, position, or orientation. Critical for reading different fonts.

Visual Memory

Ability to remember visual information and recall it later. Important for copying tasks and spelling.

Visual Sequential Memory

Ability to remember sequences of visual stimuli in order. Critical for daily living skills and following instructions.

Visual Spatial Skills

Ability to understand spatial relationships and navigate space. Important for map reading and sports.

Visual Neglect

Assessment of visual field awareness and attention to both sides of space. Important for post-stroke screening.

Figure Ground

Ability to distinguish objects from their background. Essential for visual scanning and reading.

Motion Perception

Ability to detect and track moving objects. Important for sports participation and safe navigation.

Pursuit & Fixation

Eye movement control and visual attention. Assesses oculomotor control for reading and attention.

Observation Tasks

Structured behavioral observation for comprehensive functional vision profiling and environmental assessment.

How Results Should Be Used

Clinical Disclaimer

Percepto is a visual perception screening and profiling aid. It is not a diagnostic device. Results should be interpreted by a qualified clinician. These assessments are designed for screening purposes only and do not replace comprehensive clinical evaluation.

Percepto assessments provide valuable screening data that can:

  • Identify areas of visual processing strength and challenge
  • Track progress over time with quantitative metrics
  • Support clinical decision-making and intervention planning
  • Provide objective data for educational and therapeutic planning
  • Complement, but not replace, comprehensive clinical evaluation

Current Status

Percepto is currently a pilot-stage prototype. It is suitable for demonstrations, research studies with ethics approval, and small-scale pilot programs.

Not Clinically Validated

  • No normative data established
  • Not validated against gold-standard assessments
  • Not peer-reviewed through clinical publication
  • Not regulatory approved (CE, FDA, etc.)

Technical Capabilities

  • 33 assessments operational across 11 categories
  • Firebase integration for authentication and data storage
  • Reporting system with progress tracking
  • Multi-user support for doctors and patients

Clinical validation studies are planned. The platform is ready for pilot testing to gather feedback and validate usability.
