## Notes for Developers

### Adding New Assessments
1. Follow naming convention: [CategoryPrefix][Number].html
2. Include Firebase initialization
3. Add quiz type to reports.html mapping
4. Link from appropriate menu page
5. Update this inventory

### Assessment Categories Explained
- **Visual Discrimination:** Differentiating between similar visual stimuli
- **Visual Closure:** Recognizing incomplete or obscured forms
- **Visual Form Constancy:** Recognizing forms regardless of presentation changes
- **Visual Memory:** Short-term visual recall
- **Visual Sequential Memory:** Ordered visual recall
- **Visual Spatial:** Understanding position and space
- **Visual Neglect:** Awareness of visual field
- **Figure Ground:** Separating target from background
- **Motion Perception:** Detecting and tracking movement
- **Pursuit & Fixation:** Eye movement control

### Assessment Levels
Most categories have 2 levels:
- **Level 1:** Introduction, simpler stimuli, more support
- **Level 2:** Advanced, more complex stimuli, less scaffolding
