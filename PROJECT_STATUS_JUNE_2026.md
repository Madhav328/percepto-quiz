- VC4 has inappropriate images
- Missing some planned features

**Blockers:**
- ❌ Clinical validation studies not completed
- ❌ No normative reference data
- ❌ Not published/peer-reviewed
- ❌ Regulatory status: Not a medical device
- ❌ Image content not fully verified

**Timeline for Deployment:**
- Minimum 1-2 years for clinical validation
- Requires formal studies with ethics approval
- Needs regulatory pathway determination
- Asset replacement and verification needed

**Recommendation:** NOT suitable for real patient deployment outside controlled research settings.
